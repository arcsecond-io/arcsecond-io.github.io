Arcsecond.local ​

Arcsecond.local is the full Arcsecond platform, self-hosted on your own infrastructure. It is the off-the-shelf, multi-user and multi-telescope, industry-grade platform dedicated to astronomical observatories — the whole system, running on your machines, under your control, with your data never leaving them.

Everything lives in four apps:

  • Night Studio — prepare your observations.
  • Control Room — acquire new data and automate your nights.
  • Data Grand Central — store, package and share your data.
  • Observatory Headquarters — every install is multi-user by design, so running your observatory as a team comes built in.

Contact us at team@arcsecond.io to learn more about Arcsecond.local and how to get started.

Self-Hosting Guides ​

Operating Arcsecond.local is done through the arcsecond command-line tool (see the CLI documentation). The following guides cover the most common self-hosting tasks:

  • Alpaca Server on the LAN: connect Arcsecond.local to an Alpaca server running on another Windows machine.
  • Live-Image Proxy: stream USB webcams, all-sky cameras and network cameras into Arcsecond.local.
  • Backups: where the Postgres dumps live, and how to list, inspect, and restore them.
  • Rotate Postgres Password: rotate the database password on an existing install.
  • Transient Alerts: how to setup transient alert service
