# Night Studio

> Powerful visualisations to prepare your observations.

**Night Studio** is the app where you prepare your observations. It gathers
everything Arcsecond.local offers to explore the nights ahead — decide what to
observe, when and from where, for any type of target, moving or not — and to
build the target lists and finding charts you'll hand over to the
[Control Room](/apps/control-room/).

## Night Explorer

<ThemedImage prefix="platform62-nightstudio-nightexplorer" alt="Night Explorer"/>

Our flagship tool, the Night Explorer (a.k.a. iObserve), the best
decision-making tool for planning and scheduling observations. Used by many
astronomers worldwide.

## Transit Browser

<ThemedImage prefix="platform63-nightstudio-transitsbrowser" alt="Transit Browser"/>

Our Transit Browser has been extended! It lets you browse the currently
accessible transits for exoplanets, at a given time and location, as well as
non-regular transits and other transiting objects.

## Target Lists

<ThemedImage prefix="platform62-nightstudio-targetlists" alt="Target Lists"/>

The Target Lists are a simple way to organise and manage your observing targets.
They can be used to plan observations, track progress, and share with
colleagues. They are also scriptable from the
[CLI](/cli/resources#target-lists), which is handy for importing candidates from
an external source.

## Finding Chart Editor

<ThemedImage prefix="platform63-nightstudio-fdceditor" alt="Finding Chart Editor"/>

Our Finding Chart Editor is a powerful way to prepare detailed and highly
customised finding charts, with custom overlays — from large instruments or even
your own.
