# The Apps

Arcsecond.local brings the whole life of an astronomical observatory into a
single, self-hosted platform, organised into **four apps**. The first three
follow the three phases of the observation lifecycle; the fourth is what every
multi-user install gets for free.

- **[Night Studio](#night-studio)** — prepare observations.
- **[Control Room](#control-room)** — acquire new data and automate your nights.
- **[Data Grand Central](#data-grand-central)** — store, package and share your data.
- **[Observatory Headquarters](#observatory-headquarters)** — run your observatory as a team.

## Night Studio

[Night Studio](/apps/night-studio/) gathers everything Arcsecond.local offers to
prepare your observations — powerful visualisations to decide what to observe,
when and from where.

### <span style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; gap: 0.5rem;"><img src="/icons/icon-telescope.svg" width="40" /><span>Night Explorer</span></span>

[Night Explorer](/apps/night-studio/#night-explorer), a.k.a. **iObserve**, our
flagship decision-making tool for planning and scheduling observations. Used by
many astronomers worldwide.

### <span style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; gap: 0.5rem;"><img src="/icons/icon-exoplanet.svg" width="40" /><span>Transit Browser</span></span>

The [Transit Browser](/apps/night-studio/#transit-browser) lets you browse the
currently accessible exoplanet transits for any time and location, plus
non-regular transits and other transiting objects.

### <span style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; gap: 0.5rem;"><img src="/icons/icon-targetlist.svg" width="40"/> Target Lists</span>

[Target Lists](/apps/night-studio/#target-lists) are a simple way to organise
and manage your observing targets — to plan observations, track progress, and
share with colleagues.

### <span style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; gap: 0.5rem;"><img src="/icons/icon-star.svg" width="40"/> Finding Chart Editor</span>

The [Finding Chart Editor](/apps/night-studio/#finding-chart-editor) prepares
detailed, highly customised finding charts, with custom overlays from large
instruments or even your own.

## Control Room

The [Control Room](/apps/control-room/) is the app to acquire new data and
automate your observing nights, talking directly to the equipment on (or
reachable from) your host.

It covers **Equipment Setup**, the **Observer Console**, the **Night
Scheduler**, the **Observing Queue** and the **Calibration Manager**. It is the
most recent of the four apps and under active development.

<br/><br/>

## Data Grand Central

[Data Grand Central](/apps/data/) stores, organises, packages and shares your
data — on **your own infrastructure**, with no cloud tier and no storage quota.

### <span style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; gap: 0.5rem;"><img src="/icons/icon-dataset.svg" width="40"/> Data Management</span>

[Datasets](/apps/data/datasets) are the virtual folders containing data. They
group (and let you easily download) data files that share a common source or
interest.

[Storage](/apps/data/storage) is where your data lives on the host. [External
Storages](/apps/data/external-storages) let you attach data you already own
(S3, Dropbox, FTP/SFTP, local disk, observatory archives), read-only, so
Arcsecond.local indexes it alongside its own data — consolidating your Follow-Up
Targets and reconstructing past Night Logs.

### <span style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; gap: 0.5rem;"><img src="/icons/icon-datapackage.svg" width="40"/> Data Packages</span>

[Data Packages](/apps/data/datapackages) are custom packages of Datasets to be
shared — within your organisation (e.g. for visiting observers), with other
astronomers, or with the public.

### <span style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; gap: 0.5rem;"><img src="/icons/icon-datafile.svg" width="40"/> Data & File Browser</span>

[File Browser](/apps/data/filebrowser) browses every data file known to the
install, whether stored locally or on an attached External Storage.

### <span style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; gap: 0.5rem;"><img src="/icons/icon-star.svg" width="40"/> Follow-Up Targets</span>

[Follow-Up Targets](/apps/followup/) automatically tracks every target you have
observed. Arcsecond.local maintains the list from your Night Logs and the ones it
reconstructs from your stored and attached data.

<br/><br/>

## Observatory Headquarters

Every Arcsecond.local install is **multi-user by design**.
[Observatory Headquarters](/apps/headquarters/) is the app that manages that
shared life: an unlimited number of members, a clear
[5-level permission scheme](/apps/headquarters/permissions), data sharing, and
an observatory-wide schedule and history.
