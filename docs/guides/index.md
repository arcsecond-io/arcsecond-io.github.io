# Guides

**Arcsecond.local is the full Arcsecond platform, self-hosted on your own infrastructure**: multi-user,
multi-telescope, and dedicated to astronomical observatories. It runs on your machines, under your control, and your
images and files stay on them. Its tools are grouped in [four apps](./observing/).

These guides take one problem per page, sorted by who has it.

- **Operating** the installation — [updates](./operating/updates), [backups](./operating/backups),
  [troubleshooting](./operating/troubleshooting), the [live-image proxy](./operating/live-image-proxy),
  [transient alerts](./operating/transient-alerts), an [Alpaca server on another machine](./operating/alpaca-lan),
  [rotating the database password](./operating/rotate-postgres-password).
- **Observing** — [the four apps](./observing/): [Night Studio](./observing/night-studio), the
  [Control Room](./observing/control-room) and its seven tools, [Data Grand Central](./data/) and
  [Observatory Headquarters](./team/).
- **Data** — [storage](./data/storage), [external storages](./data/external-storages), [datasets](./data/datasets),
  [data packages](./data/datapackages), the [file browser](./data/filebrowser), [credentials security](./data/credentials-security).
- **Team** — [permissions and roles](./team/permissions).

Getting an installation running in the first place is [Start](/start/).
Questions? Write to [team@arcsecond.io](mailto:team@arcsecond.io).
