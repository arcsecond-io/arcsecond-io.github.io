Safety Configuration ​

Know when to stop, and stop by itself. Conditions are evaluated continuously while observing, and a procedure makes the observatory safe when they fail.

Condition sets ​

A condition set watches a site, a telescope or a target, and gives a verdict: GO or NOGO. It is NOGO when any of its conditions matches, or only when all do, as you choose.

Arcsecond.local starts with these:

SetFor eachWhat it guards
Weather SafetySiteWind, humidity, clouds, rain, and the safety monitor where there is one
Night BoundsSiteDaylight: the night ends at dawn
Mount LimitsTelescopeThe lowest altitude, and the telescope's horizon mask
Sun AvoidanceTelescopeThe distance to the Sun. Mandatory: it cannot be disabled or deleted, and its separation can only be raised

Review their thresholds for your own site before relying on them.

A disabled set is still evaluated and logged, but nothing acts on its verdict.

Procedures ​

A procedure is what a set runs on NOGO: steps run one after the other, and the tasks of a step in sequence or together. For each task, choose whether a failure aborts the procedure or is ignored. A procedure always starts by aborting what is running, so that nothing keeps commanding the devices it is closing.

Each site starts with two:

  • Fail-Safe Shutdown parks the mount, and closes the cover and the dome. It is also what Emergency Stop launches in the Observer Console.
  • Reopen does the opposite, and is only ever launched by hand.

Safety never reopens

Nothing runs when conditions return to GO. Once closed, the observatory stays closed until someone reopens it.

DANGER

Check that every procedure names the right devices, and test it while you are at the telescope. Arcsecond's safety does not replace hardware protections, such as limit switches and a rain sensor wired to the dome.

Decisions Log ​

Every change between GO and NOGO is kept with the conditions that caused it and the procedure that ran.

Only admins and owners edit conditions and procedures. Everyone else can read them, and the log.
