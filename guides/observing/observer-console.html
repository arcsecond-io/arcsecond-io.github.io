On this page

Observer Console ​

A whole night by hand, in one frame. Point, focus, guide, expose and review without changing screen.

What it is for ​

  • Observe manually with one setup.
  • Take a queue of exposures, or a mosaic, on the current target.
  • Inspect frames as they land, with guiding and sky conditions in view.

For a night that runs on its own, use the Night Scheduler.

Reading the console ​

From top to bottom:

AreaWhat it shows
NightThe airmass curve of the target with the time and the hourly weather, the sky as the telescope sees it, its coordinates and the state of the dome
Session controlsTarget, telescope, camera, filter wheel, rotator, focuser and dome, each with its commands
Safety lineThe current GO or NOGO, and the Emergency Stop
PreviewThe Queue and Review tabs, above a gallery of the night's frames
Side panelsGuiding, sky conditions and equipment parameters, opened as needed

A typical observation ​

  1. Choose the observing setup in the status bar and take control of it.
  2. Pick a target, enter coordinates, or take one from a target list.
  3. Slew, then Center... to refine the pointing by plate solving.
  4. Choose a filter, and run Find Focus....
  5. Start guiding from the Guiding panel.
  6. Set the exposure time and count, then Start.

What is checked before an exposure ​

The console stops and asks rather than take a frame that would be lost:

It finds that…It proposes to…
The telescope is off targetSlew first, then start, or start anyway
A moving target will trail during the exposureShorten it, or expose anyway. Non-sidereal tracking makes the mount follow the target
The exposure would cross the meridianFlip, then start
The sequence will reach the meridianFlip automatically when it is due, and carry on

Queue and mosaics ​

In the Queue tab, each row is a series of exposures with its filter and rotator angle. Run queue takes them in order. The field is shown beside the queue, on your finding charts or on Aladin.

Add a mosaic lays out a grid of tiles on the field: choose the grid, the overlap, the scan order and the filters. Each tile becomes a row, and what is drawn is exactly what is shot.

Review ​

The Review tab opens a frame as soon as it arrives: scale, colour map, background, noise, saturation, number of stars, HFR and FWHM, a close-up, and the full FITS header. The gallery is filtered by kind: science, calibration, autofocus, plate solving.

Safety ​

The safety line shows the verdict of each condition set (see Safety Configuration). Emergency Stop launches the site's Fail-Safe Shutdown. When the line says the verdict is unknown, nothing has been evaluated in the last hour: it does not mean that all is well.
