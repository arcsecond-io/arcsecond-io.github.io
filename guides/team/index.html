Observatory Headquarters ​

Every Arcsecond.local installation is multi-user by design. You do not opt into a separate "organisation" or "portal" product — the moment you run Arcsecond.local, you are running a shared space for your whole observatory, science group, university, planetarium or astronomy club. Observatory Headquarters is the app where that shared life is managed.

"All astronomical observatories in the world have in common the needs to prepare and plan observations, organise and execute night operations, then store, package and distribute data."

— Cédric Foellmi, Ph.D. (former ESO Fellow in La Silla, and Arcsecond Founder)

The other three apps — Night Studio, the Control Room and Data Grand Central — are the same for every member of your install. Observatory Headquarters adds the organisation-wide layer on top of them.

What it gives you ​

  • A single home for the whole life of your observatory — the history of all observations, observers, targets and observing nights, and a modern data archive built on the same APIs that power the rest of the platform, even for data located elsewhere (see External Storages).
  • An unlimited number of members, with four clear roles (Permissions & Roles) to control who can do what and who can access what.
  • Sharing, from a private dataset between two members up to a customizable public page exposing selected data to the world.
  • An observatory schedule, a map, storages and settings shared by everyone, each with its own tool.

Members and roles ​

Membership and access are governed by four roles, Visitor, Member, Admin and Owner, on top of what anyone may see in public. What each role is for is on the Permissions & Roles page, and the complete list of rights, generated from the server, on the Roles and permissions page.

The tools ​

Schedule ​

One calendar for the whole observatory. Nights fill in by themselves from your data, beside the events you add: maintenance, meetings, visitors. Events can recur, and name telescopes, observers and night operators.

Map ​

A drawing of the observatory and of how it is linked: sites, domes, buildings, rooms, telescopes, equipment, computers, software and network. It starts from your registry; admins arrange it. Members only.

Data Storages ​

Where the data lives, and how it gets there. Admins attach storages (SMB, S3, SFTP...) to which new data files and database backups are pushed. See External storages.

Settings ​

Activation, email, file naming, appearance and preferences. Admins only.
